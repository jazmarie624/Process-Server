Page 2 — the two things that get service quashed

Substitute Service

All three or it fails · card 3 of 8
3 PRONGS OR NOTHING
  • 1 · Usual place of abodeWhere they actually live right now. Not a mailing address, not last month's apartment.
    "Does [NAME] live here?"
    "¿Vive aquí [NOMBRE]?"
  • 2 · Resident, 15 or olderThey must live there. A visiting relative, babysitter, or contractor does NOT count.
    "Do you live here too? Are you 15 or older?"
    "¿Usted también vive aquí? ¿Tiene quince años o más?"
  • 3 · Informed of contentsYou must say it out loud and swear to it.
    "These are legal documents for [NAME] — a summons and complaint."
    "Son documentos legales — una citación y una demanda."
Then get their name, relationship, and age for the return.

At the Door

Every serve · card 4 of 8
  • Scan: AC running, mail, trash cans, tags, blinds, dog, camera
  • Photograph every vehicle tag
  • Park nose-out, never blocking a driveway
  • ID visible, papers folded low in non-dominant hand
  • Stand hinge side, one step back
  • Three firm knocks + doorbell
  • Confirm identity before handing anything over
  • If substitute — run all 3 prongs (card 3)
  • Get the name and relationship of who you served
  • Write date, time, initials, CPS# on page 1
  • Leave. No explaining, no legal questions
  • Exact time noted before you reach the car
Abort & log an attempt if: loose dog, unconfirmed identity through a closed door, they say they don't live there, or anything feels wrong.

Page 3 — hand these to guards and managers

Notice to Gate Staff & Employers

English · hand this over · card 5 of 8
Gated communities — Fla. Stat. §48.031(7) A gated residential community, including a condominium association or cooperative, must grant a process server unannounced entry to serve a defendant or witness residing inside. The community may not announce the server's arrival and may not deny entry.
Employers — Fla. Stat. §48.031(1)(b) An employer must allow a process server to serve an employee in a private area designated by the employer. Refusing is a noncriminal violation punishable by a fine of up to $1,000.

I am not law enforcement and I am not here to cause a disruption. I am a process server certified by the Ninth Judicial Circuit. I am happy to do this as discreetly as possible.

If entry is still refused, please provide your name and your company name for the court record.
Jazmin Aguayo · CPS #_______J Empire Server

Page 4 — when it goes wrong, and before you sign

What To Do If

You won't think clearly in the moment · card 7 of 8
  • Someone threatens youLeave immediately — backward two steps, turn, go. Call 911 and file a report. Tell the client same day. Flag the address. Never return alone.
  • A dog is looseNo attempt. Log it: "Attempt aborted — unsecured dog." Billable and documented. If charged: stand still, turn sideways. Don't run.
  • Police stop youStay calm, hands visible. "I'm a certified process server serving civil papers." Show your circuit ID. You are not doing anything unlawful.
  • You served the wrong personDo not sign the return. Call the attorney today and say so plainly. An honest re-attempt costs $20. A false return is a felony and a lifetime ban.
  • Your car dies mid-routePapers stay with you, never in a towed car. Photograph the odometer and location for your mileage log.
  • You're unsure the serve was goodDon't sign. Call your mentor or the attorney. Uncertainty is a re-attempt, not a signature.
911 · then client · then flag itWhen in doubt, don't sign

Before You Sign

Return of service audit · card 8 of 8
  • Date and time the process came to hand
  • Date and time served, to the minute
  • Manner — personal / substitute / posting / corporate
  • Full name of person served, spelled right
  • Their capacity, if representative
  • Every document listed by name
  • Complete address including unit
  • If substitute — all 3 prongs written out, with age
  • If eviction posting — both times, 6+ hours apart
  • If unknown occupants — 3 attempts: business hours, non-business, weekend
  • Your signature and CPS number
  • Client app matches: attempts, photos, GPS, status
